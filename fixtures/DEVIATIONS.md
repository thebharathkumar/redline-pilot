# Counterparty deviations

fixtures/counterparty_nda.docx is fixtures/common_paper_nda.docx with these changes, written by
scripts/make_fixtures.py. Each row names the playbook rule it should trigger.

## 1. ci-marking (Section 1)

Base: which (1) the Disclosing Party identifies to the receiving party (“Receiving Party”) as “confidential”, “proprietary”, or the like or (2) should be reasonably understood as confidential or proprietary due to its nature and the circumstances of its disclosure

Counterparty: which is marked “confidential” or “proprietary” at the time of disclosure to the receiving party (“Receiving Party”) and, if disclosed orally, is summarized in writing and marked as confidential within ten (10) days of disclosure

## 2. ci-scope (Section 1)

Base: Confidential Information includes technical or business information, product designs or roadmaps, requirements, pricing, security and compliance documentation, technology, inventions and know-how.

Counterparty: Confidential Information is limited to technical information, source code and trade secrets.

## 3. disc-advisors (Section 2(b))

Base: to its employees, agents, advisors, contractors and other representatives having

Counterparty: to its employees having

## 4. disc-legal (Section 4)

Base: reasonably cooperates, at the Disclosing Party’s expense, with the Disclosing Party’s efforts

Counterparty: uses best efforts, at the Receiving Party’s expense, to contest the requirement and to support the Disclosing Party’s efforts

## 5. term-length (Cover Page, Term of Confidentiality)

Base: 1 year from Effective Date, but in the case of trade secrets until Confidential Information is no longer considered a trade secret under applicable laws.

Counterparty: In perpetuity.

## 6. ret-retention (Section 6)

Base: As an exception to subsection (b), the Receiving Party may retain Confidential Information in accordance with its standard backup or record retention policies or as required by law, but the terms of this MNDA will continue to apply to the retained Confidential Information.

Counterparty: The Receiving Party will permanently delete all Confidential Information, including copies held in electronic backup systems.

## 7. rem-equitable (Section 10)

Base: the Disclosing Party is entitled to seek appropriate equitable relief, including an injunction, in addition to its other remedies.

Counterparty: the Disclosing Party is entitled to an injunction without posting a bond, and the Receiving Party will pay liquidated damages of $50,000 for each breach, in addition to the Disclosing Party’s other remedies.

## 8. law-venue (Cover Page, Governing Law and Jurisdiction)

Base: Courts located in New York County, New York.

Counterparty: Courts located in Travis County, Texas.

Base: Governing Law | New York

Counterparty: Governing Law | Texas
